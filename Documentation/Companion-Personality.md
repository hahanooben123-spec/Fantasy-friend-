# Companion personality and continuity

## Shared reactions

LivingFriend can greet on arrival, look towards a work point, and receive curious,
proud or welcome moments independently of the six explicit playground actions.
Three new authored rig clips provide a head boop, curious lean and happy clapping.
The existing paired limb anchors remain the animation pivots.

A tap high on the friend triggers a squash and wink; dragging over it tickles.
Other taps select a different wave, proud response, hug, jump or curious lean.
VoiceOver retains a direct hug action. All sound remains short musical reactions,
without speech synthesis. Clips use the existing paused clock and Reduce Motion.

## Village

The home companion waves on arrival. Choosing a house first walks it across the
actual map for 0.72 seconds. The existing short doorway transition completes the
visit. Navigation is locked during departure so multiple houses cannot queue.
With Reduce Motion the map walk is skipped. Departure tasks cancel when the view
leaves; the doorway remains skippable by tapping.

## Pizza

A small companion watches the finger, ingredients and dough throughout preparation
and baking. Pouring prompts curiosity, completing ingredients or first shaping
prompts delight, and the serving reveal prompts proud clapping. During eating,
the real pizza mouthful and existing chew animation remain in use.

## Creations in the room

The room shares its PumpkinStore with the workshop. Up to two saved lanterns are
large picture buttons that open the chosen lantern in the workshop. A served pizza
for this friend appears on a table, or on the floor if there is no table. Tapping
continues eating the same saved recipe; consumed food is not silently recreated.
Creation buttons are disabled while arranging furniture. The village keeps its
existing personal keepsakes beside the activity houses.

No build, tests or simulator checks were run, as requested.
