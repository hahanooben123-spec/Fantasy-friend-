## Runtime integration

`Components/PaintedPizzaArt.swift` provides the illustrated kitchen, live oven flames, dynamic dough/sauce materials and serving steam. The existing SwiftPM/main-bundle image loader handles both project formats. Ingredients are shared by tray, dragging and pouring. The transparent water jug has a separate clipped water level. Bowl contents remain dynamic, with the painted front lip redrawn above them and the moving wooden spoon. Oven fire is rendered inside the illustrated chamber; the child's actual pizza remains a separate live layer. The plate is drawn outside the pizza's bite mask, so finger bites remove food rather than ceramic. Free dough shapes, sauce strokes, unlimited toppings and finger-position bites retain their existing model and input paths.

Original procedural toppings and herbs remain dynamic. Dough has warm bread shading and subtle deterministic pores; sauce has layered warm shading and gloss. Existing cheese pools and browned spots continue to follow baking progress. Fire and steam follow scene activity, game pause and Reduce Motion.

The image outputs were viewed as returned by image_gen. No build, tests, UI screenshots, device checks or visual verification of the running app were performed.
