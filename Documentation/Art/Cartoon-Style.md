# FantasiVen: cute cartoon direction

User correction, 24 October 2026: the current detailed painted environments have drifted away from the supplied cute cartoon references. The replacement set is now integrated in the game. See Cartoon-Art.md for saved files and final prompts.

## Visual priorities

- Original cuddly characters with large expressive eyes, short rounded limbs and readable silhouettes.
- Bright lime, turquoise, coral, orange and purple. Use quieter large colour areas behind controls.
- Smooth 2D cartoon shapes with gentle volume, clear outlines and a few broad highlights.
- Big soft trees, cloud shapes and toy-like buildings. Keep the central character visually dominant.
- Props should be recognisable at actual phone size. Remove tiny hardware, ornament and wood grain.
- Playful squash, stretch, anticipation, blinks and reactions should carry the personality.
- Use the supplied Om Nom / Where's My Water references for readability, expressive proportions and clear mobile game presentation, without copying their characters or assets.

## Avoid

- Realistic grass, rock, leaf, food or timber textures.
- Dense fantasy scenery, miniature architectural detail and elaborate metallic trim.
- Heavy brown frames, dim overall lighting and a uniform muted palette.
- Mixing a flat native character with highly realistic painted scenery.
- Treating increased saturation or additional particles as a substitute for cohesive illustration.

Keep artwork and interactive layers separate, preserve the child's saved friends and creations, and verify a representative composition at phone and iPad sizes before propagating the style to every scene.
