# Painted village production art

Created using the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-24. These are production assets used by the app, not screenshots or static UI mockups. No reference images or third-party character designs were provided to the generator. Originals remain in the Codex generated-images folder; project copies are bundled in the asset catalog.

## Assets and integration

All six images live in `FantasiVen.swiftpm/Resources/Assets.xcassets/`, as `VillageEnvironment.imageset/artwork.png`, `VillageStudio.imageset/artwork.png`, `VillagePizza.imageset/artwork.png`, `VillageGames.imageset/artwork.png`, `VillageHomes.imageset/artwork.png`, and `VillagePumpkins.imageset/artwork.png`. The five building PNGs retain their generated alpha transparency. No bitmap editing or resizing was applied.

`PaintedVillageArt.swift` loads images from Bundle.module in SwiftPM and the main bundle in Xcode. The square environment is cropped to fill the viewport. Buildings retain their square aspect ratio. Doors are separately masked from the same sprite and hinged in 3D; warm interior light appears behind them. Each doorway has authored normalized bounds. Room colors appear on a small porch rug instead of recoloring the entire painted building. Labels remain localized SwiftUI text. Friends and saved creations remain separate interactive content.

The environment is shared by the village, welcome panels, journeys and the forest/meadow worlds. Fine light motes update at 12 FPS only when active and respect Reduce Motion. Thumbnails use the still environment. A warm panel protects header text. Asset work was inspected as returned by image_gen; no app build, UI QA, device rendering or tests were performed.
